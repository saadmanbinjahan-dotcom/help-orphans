Our Mission

We want to support vulnerable children and people without stable housing by collecting voluntary donations and using them for practical assistance such as meals, clothing, hygiene items and other essential needs.

Every contribution matters. Even a small donation can become a meal, a warm piece of clothing, or an essential item for someone in need.
